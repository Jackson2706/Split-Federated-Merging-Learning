\begin{abstract}
Hierarchical split-federated learning reduces device-side computation by assigning consecutive model segments to clients, edges, and a cloud. H-SFP further decouples these tiers: a lower tier transmits class-conditional feature statistics from which the next tier synthesizes training inputs. Its single diagonal Gaussian per class, however, preserves only marginal first and second moments. A multimodal class may therefore be represented by samples in low-density gaps, while repeated aggregation can erase modes contributed within or across clients. We introduce \method{}, a code-grounded extension that communicates class-conditional mixtures of diagonal-Gaussian components. Clients obtain components by seeded clustering with fixed or held-out-likelihood-selected component counts; edges retain components from different clients, train on mixture samples, re-estimate output-space summaries after their nonlinear segment, and greedily merge same-class components subject to component or serialized-byte budgets. We prove exact preservation of mass, mean, and diagonal second moment for every merge, derive packet and computation costs, and state a conditional fixed-model loss bound without claiming optimization convergence. The evaluation protocol separates within-client multimodality, across-client multimodality, encoder-coordinate mismatch, representation fidelity, predictive quality, and communication. On CIFAR-100 with 200 clients and five edges (31 configurations, three seeds each, 93 validated runs), retaining within- and across-client components raised final-round test accuracy under Dir($\alpha{=}0.1$) from $12.66\pm0.36\%$ for H-SFP to $15.92\pm0.16\%$ for MiSFP-K2 and $16.63\pm0.31\%$ for MiSFP-K4, at $4$--$5\%$ more total communication; a byte-matched float16 K2 packet kept the gain. Absolute accuracy is low for every method under the 60-round budget, the held-out-likelihood adaptive rule added few components, and adapted FedAvg, SplitFed, and FedProto baselines have not been run. All reported values are generated from a validated result manifest.
\end{abstract}
