\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\ehsfp{} extends hierarchical split-federated prototyping to upper tiers that train from partial, delayed, and masked class summaries by combining timestamped moment replay, training-time source masking, learned source weighting, prototype replay consistency, and optional shared full-covariance synthesis. Formulating synthesis as an operator pipeline isolates how each mechanism shapes the training law: replay trades coverage for drift bounded linearly in the memory weight when inputs are fresh, masking drops classes held by few sources, learned weighting shifts the synthetic mean only when sources disagree, and contribution age enters drift quadratically under bounded pipeline sensitivity. Theorem~\ref{thm:convergence} embeds the resulting distributional drift as an explicit residual in a tier-wise stationarity bound. Across five-run, 200-round evaluations, \ehsfp{} attains 56.07\% mean accuracy on CIFAR-100 and 69.12\% mean IoU and 80.23\% mean Dice on ISIC-2018 while maintaining robustness under masking, staleness, and simulated execution disruptions. Future work should benchmark physical edge--cloud deployment overhead, mitigate severe label skew via multi-prototype synthesis or shared negative anchors, and connect tier-wise surrogate stationarity to the composed global prediction risk~\eqref{eq:global_objective}.
