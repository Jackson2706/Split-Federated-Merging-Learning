\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\ehsfp{} extends hierarchical split-federated prototyping to upper tiers that train from partial, delayed, and masked class summaries, using moment replay, learned source weighting, source masking, and replay consistency. Writing synthesis as an operator pipeline shows that each mechanism acts on a distinct quantity: replay adds drift at most linearly in the memory weight, masking removes classes held by few sources, and learned weighting moves the synthetic mean only when sources disagree. Theorem~\ref{thm:convergence} turns the resulting drift into an explicit residual in a tier-wise stationarity bound. In five-run, 200-round simulations, \ehsfp{} reaches 56.07\% mean accuracy on CIFAR-100 and 69.12\% mean IoU and 80.23\% mean Dice on ISIC-2018. Future work should measure drift and coverage directly, address severe label skew, and link tier-wise stationarity to the composed prediction risk.
