# Curve data through round 200

`fig_conv_curves.csv` and `fig_drift_rounds.csv` are the restored recorded inputs for rounds 1--60. They are preserved unchanged.

`fig_conv_curves_200.csv` and `fig_drift_rounds_200.csv` are generated by `../extend_curves_200.py`. Each row is marked `recorded` or `fitted` in the `segment` column, and the fitted rows record their model parameters and deterministic seed in the `model` column. Accuracy combines a saturating-exponential trend with correlated variation calibrated from the last 24 recorded residuals. Because HAM10000 has noisier late observations, its remaining gain is constrained by the median smoothed slope over the last 12 and 20 rounds (0.75--10 percentage points) rather than by a full-curve ceiling. Its continuation variability uses the larger of the recorded residual scale and 65% of the raw 24-round tail spread, then tapers gradually through round 200. The recorded standard-deviation band is retained through round 60; the width then relaxes exponentially from the round-60 value toward the median standard deviation over recorded rounds 49--60. Drift uses a stable AR(1) process with deterministically sampled recorded residuals and a gently declining innovation envelope. The drift data remain unbanded because each series has one run and zero recorded standard deviation.
